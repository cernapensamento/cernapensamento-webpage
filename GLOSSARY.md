# Cerna Pensamento

Digital cultural journal and intellectual magazine published in Galician (`gl`) and Spanish (`es`).

## Language

**Tag**:
A localized topical classification assigned to a publication for discovery and archive filtering.
_Avoid_: Label, Theme

**Editorial Board** (*Consello Editorial* / *Consejo Editorial*):
The reviewing body that evaluates author proposals, grants Guest Author access, and certifies finished manuscripts for publication.
_Avoid_: Central Committee, Comité Central, Comité Editorial

**Guest Author** (*Autor Convidado* / *Autor Invitado*):
An external contributor granted access to the Author Desk to draft and submit manuscripts for Editorial Board review, capped at 2 publications per year and 4 in total.
_Avoid_: Invite Author, Invited Member

**Bilingual Publication**:
A publication authored and published in both Galician (`gl`) and Spanish (`es`), with one language designated as its original language.
_Avoid_: Dual-language article, Full translation

**Monolingual Publication**:
A publication authored and published in a single language (either Galician or Spanish), still discoverable across both site locales in its original language.
_Avoid_: Single-locale post, Untranslated draft
